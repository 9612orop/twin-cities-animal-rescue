Organization Story

Founded in 2012, Twin Cities Animal Rescue began as a grassroots shelter network determined to provide immediate relief and medical care to abandoned domestic pets.

Core Values

  • Compassion: Every rescue animal receives unconditional dignity, respect, and medical care.
  • Transparency: We keep honest communication with our donors, volunteers, and foster families.
  • Community Outreach: Public education remains central to preventing future animal abandonment.

Partner Acknowledgments

Our work is powered by partnerships with veterinary clinics, local feed suppliers, and individual sponsors across the metropolitan area.
